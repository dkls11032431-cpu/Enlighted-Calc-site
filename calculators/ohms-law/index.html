Formula reference

Ohm’s law

V = I × R

Voltage equals current multiplied by resistance.

Electrical power

P = V × I = I²R = V²/R

For a resistive DC circuit.

Assumption: steady-state DC or a purely resistive circuit. For AC loads with phase angle, use real power P = V × I × PF (single-phase) or √3 × VLL × I × PF (balanced three-phase). Do not use this simple solver as an AC motor or power-system analysis tool.
