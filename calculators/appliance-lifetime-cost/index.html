Important assumptions: This is a simple, undiscounted estimate. Annual electricity use and maintenance are assumed constant; the tariff increase compounds once per year if entered. The calculator excludes financing, inflation on maintenance, repair shocks, resale value, replacement, taxes, fixed charges and changes in usage. Enter annual kWh figures only when the test basis and model variant are comparable. BEE label values may use defined test conditions and may not equal your household's actual consumption.

How to get reliable inputs

  • Match the complete model number and variant on the product label and seller quotation.
  • Use annual energy consumption from the current applicable BEE label or official manufacturer documentation.
  • Use current purchase quotations and note delivery/installation charges separately.
  • Use a realistic maintenance estimate based on warranty terms, consumables and local service quotes.
