01. Rooftop Solar System Sizing

Estimate preliminary PV capacity and panel count from monthly electricity consumption and site assumptions.

Enter your monthly units and site assumptions to estimate a system.

Planning estimate only. Peak sun hours and performance ratio are user assumptions, not live location data or a generation guarantee. Final design must verify seasonal yield, roof shading, inverter DC limits, utility approval and applicable requirements.

02. Electricity Cost Calculator

Estimate energy consumption and cost for a load operated over a typical day.

Enter values and calculate.

Estimate excludes fixed charges, demand charges, taxes, slab tariffs, power-factor penalties and time-of-day adjustments.

03. Voltage Drop Estimator

Approximate single-phase copper conductor voltage drop using a simplified resistive model.

Enter values and calculate.

Simplified copper-only estimate using ρ = 0.0175 Ω·mm²/m at approximately 20°C and a two-conductor path. It excludes reactance, operating temperature, joints and installation conditions. Not a cable-sizing approval.

04. DG Fuel Consumption

Estimate diesel use from measured or assumed specific fuel consumption.

Enter values and calculate.

Fuel consumption varies with engine model, load percentage, ambient conditions, maintenance and fuel quality. Use the OEM fuel curve or actual site log for budgeting.

05. UPS Battery Runtime

Indicative battery energy estimate for a DC battery bank and AC load.

Enter values and calculate.

Simplified energy calculation. Actual runtime can be substantially lower due to Peukert effect, battery age, temperature, discharge rate, cut-off voltage, wiring losses and UPS behaviour. Use manufacturer discharge tables for design.
