Calculation method

AC energy demandEac = Load × backup hours

Assumes constant AC load throughout the backup period.

Required nominal battery energyEbank = Eac ÷ (inverter efficiency × DoD)

DoD and efficiency are user-entered fractions.

Required Ah at DC voltageAh = Ebank(Wh) ÷ system voltage

Then round parallel strings up to whole battery strings.

ConfigurationSeries = system V ÷ battery V

Battery voltage must divide evenly into the system voltage.

Limitations: no Peukert effect, C-rate, temperature correction, ageing reserve, battery chemistry-specific voltage limits, BMS limits, cable loss, inverter surge, charge-source sizing, recharge time or cell balancing is modelled. Do not treat this estimate as a battery manufacturer's sizing approval.
