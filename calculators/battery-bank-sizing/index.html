Calculation method

AC energy requirementE = Load (W) × backup hours

Assumes constant average load.

Required nominal AhAh = E × (1 + margin) ÷ (V × DoD × η)

DoD and efficiency are entered as decimal fractions.

Series battery countNs = system voltage ÷ battery voltage

Must be a whole number of matching batteries.

Parallel stringsNp = ceiling(required Ah ÷ battery Ah)

Each string has the capacity of one battery.

Battery chemistry, discharge-rate curves (Peukert effect), inverter low-voltage cut-off, temperature, ageing, cell balancing, charging profile, recharge time, cable sizing, DC protection and manufacturer series/parallel limits are not modelled. Verify the proposed configuration against the battery and UPS manufacturer documentation and applicable electrical requirements before purchase or installation.
