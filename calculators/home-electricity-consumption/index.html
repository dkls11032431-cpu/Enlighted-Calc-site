How to improve estimate accuracy

  • Use the appliance input wattage shown on its rating plate, not cooling capacity or marketing tonnage.
  • For cycling loads such as refrigerators and inverter air conditioners, use a realistic average input over the day rather than assuming full rated power continuously.
  • Measure uncertain loads with a suitable plug-in energy meter or sub-meter where safe and appropriate.
  • Compare the estimate against actual meter readings over the same billing period and adjust operating hours or average watts.
  • Actual bills can include fixed charges, slab tariffs, taxes, fuel adjustments, demand charges and other items not represented by a single energy rate.

This calculator is for education and preliminary energy budgeting. It does not replace meter readings, utility billing calculations, electrical inspection or professional energy auditing.
