AC power relationships

For sinusoidal single-phase or balanced three-phase systems: P = S × PF; Q = √(S² − P²); S = √(P² + Q²). kVA, kW and kVAr are not interchangeable. Power factor is required to convert between real and apparent power.

Horsepower conversion uses mechanical horsepower (1 hp = 745.699872 W). Electrical motor input power depends on efficiency and power factor; this converter does not infer motor input from a nameplate HP value.
