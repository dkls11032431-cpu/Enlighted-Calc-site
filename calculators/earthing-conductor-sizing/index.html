Engineering checks still required

Confirm the actual earth-fault current at the installation point, maximum protective-device clearing time, protective-device characteristics, conductor material and insulation, initial/final temperature assumptions, parallel paths, joints and mechanical protection. Important: This is an adiabatic arithmetic check only. It does not determine fault current or clearing time, select a protective device, verify earth-fault loop impedance, touch/step voltage, conductor mechanical strength, corrosion, or minimum sizes required by the applicable current standard and local authority. Never select an earthing conductor from this result alone.
