Calculation method

DC bank voltageVbank = Vbattery × batteries in series

Series connection increases voltage, not Ah capacity.

Bank capacityAhbank = Ahbattery × parallel strings

Parallel strings increase nominal Ah capacity.

Usable AC energyWh = Vbank × Ahbank × DoD × efficiency

Both DoD and efficiency are entered as fractions.

Idealised runtimeHours = usable AC Wh ÷ AC load W

Constant load and constant efficiency are assumed.

This simple energy model does not apply Peukert correction or manufacturer discharge curves. Actual runtime can be materially lower at high discharge rates, low temperatures, aged batteries or near UPS cut-off voltage. Check battery discharge tables, UPS compatibility, DC current limits, string fusing and cable sizing before designing a bank.

Important engineering limitations

This calculator is for education and preliminary estimation. It is not a substitute for applicable Indian Standards, CEA requirements, manufacturer data, site measurements or a design prepared and checked by a qualified electrical professional. Verify all input data and assumptions before making equipment or safety decisions.
