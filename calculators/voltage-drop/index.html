Estimate cable voltage drop for copper or aluminium conductors in single-phase and balanced three-phase systems. This tool applies a simplified impedance model using your cable length, current, conductor area, temperature, power factor and reactance assumptions.

Calculation method

Single-phaseΔV = 2 × L × I × (R cosφ + X sinφ)

Two loaded conductors, with one-way route length L.

Three-phaseΔV = √3 × L × I × (R cosφ + X sinφ)

Balanced three-phase line-to-line voltage drop estimate.

Temperature correctionRθ = R20 × [1 + α(θ − 20)]

Approximate α: copper 0.00393/°C; aluminium 0.00403/°C.

Reactance inputX = entered Ω/km × L(km)

Use manufacturer or installation-specific impedance data where available.

The model assumes balanced loading for three-phase, sinusoidal current, a single conductor size and a user-supplied reactance. It excludes parallel runs, harmonics, skin/proximity effects, joints, motor starting, transient conditions and protective-device coordination. Check ampacity, installation method and short-circuit withstand separately.

Important engineering limitations

This calculator is for education and preliminary estimation. It is not a substitute for applicable Indian Standards, CEA requirements, manufacturer data, site measurements or a design prepared and checked by a qualified electrical professional. Verify all input data and assumptions before making equipment or safety decisions.
