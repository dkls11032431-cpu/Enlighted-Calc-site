How the estimate is calculated

  • Monthly solar generation = system capacity (kWp) × daily generation per kWp × days per month.
  • Self-consumed units = generation × self-consumption percentage. Their value is the avoided grid tariff.
  • Exported units = remaining generation. Their value is the export credit entered by you.
  • Annual net savings in year one = 12 × monthly energy benefit − annual maintenance.
  • Simple payback = net upfront investment ÷ first-year annual net savings. The cash-flow estimate applies the entered annual degradation and tariff escalation assumptions.

Fixed charges, demand charges, taxes, time-of-day tariffs, financing interest, inverter/battery replacement, insurance and changing policy rules are not included.
