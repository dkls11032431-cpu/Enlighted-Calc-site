Engineering interpretation

The total earth-fault loop impedance includes the source, line conductor, protective conductor and all relevant connections/return paths for the fault condition. Use a suitable calibrated loop-impedance tester and the correct test method for the installation. Enter the applicable maximum Zs only after identifying the protective device, its characteristics, rated current/setting, required disconnection time, supply conditions and the governing standard/rules.

Critical limitation: This tool performs Ohm’s-law arithmetic and a comparison against a user-supplied limit. It does not calculate or validate the permitted Zs, protective-device trip curve, disconnection time, RCD operation, minimum fault current, maximum fault level, breaking capacity, conductor thermal withstand, touch voltage or installation compliance. A PASS means only that the entered Zs is not greater than the entered limit; it is not proof of safe disconnection. Testing and interpretation must be performed by a qualified person.
