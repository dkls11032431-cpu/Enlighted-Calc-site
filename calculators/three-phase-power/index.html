Three-phase power
& energy calculator.

Calculate active power, apparent power, reactive power, energy and estimated cost for a balanced three-phase load. Use measured line-to-line voltage, line current and true power factor from a suitable power analyser or meter.

Measured operating conditions

Enter RMS line values. The calculator assumes a balanced sinusoidal three-phase system.

Use true power factor, not displacement PF, where harmonics are present. For unbalanced loads, measure each phase separately; this balanced-load calculator is not valid for phase-by-phase imbalance analysis.

Power relationship
P² + Q² = S²For sinusoidal balanced operation, Q sign depends on inductive or capacitive load.

Engineering method & formulas

Apparent powerS = √3 × VLL × IL / 1000

Uses line-to-line RMS voltage and line RMS current.

Active powerP = S × PF

Real power converted to useful work, heat or other active energy.

Reactive powerQ = √(S² − P²)

Magnitude only; the sign requires leading/lagging information.

Monthly energyE = P × hours/day × days

Assumes the entered load remains constant over the operating schedule.

Limitations: no phase imbalance, neutral current, harmonic distortion, voltage fluctuation, demand interval, maximum-demand charge, tariff slab, fixed charge, taxes or power-factor penalty is modelled. Use billing-meter data for billing reconciliation. This is an engineering estimate, not a protection or cable-sizing tool.
