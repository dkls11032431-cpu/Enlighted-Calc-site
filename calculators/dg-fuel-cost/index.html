Calculation method

Apparent loadLoad kVA = Load kW ÷ assumed PF

Uses the entered load power factor.

Fuel interpolationL/h = linear interpolation between OEM points

Interpolates the entered 25%, 50%, 75% and 100% fuel-rate values.

Daily fuelDaily litres = L/h × hours/day

Assumes a steady load and fuel rate throughout the day.

Fuel costCost = litres × diesel price

Does not include maintenance, lubricants or generator ownership cost.

The DG loading percentage is based on kVA rating and an assumed 0.8 load power factor. Actual fuel consumption depends on engine model, alternator efficiency, site conditions, maintenance, fuel quality and load profile. Below 25% or above 100% rated load, this tool does not extrapolate the fuel curve.

Important engineering limitations

This calculator is for education and preliminary estimation. It is not a substitute for applicable Indian Standards, CEA requirements, manufacturer data, site measurements or a design prepared and checked by a qualified electrical professional. Verify all input data and assumptions before making equipment or safety decisions.
