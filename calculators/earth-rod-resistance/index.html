Single rod approximation: R ≈ ρ / (2πL) × [ln(4L/d) − 1], where L and d use the same length unit. Multiple rods are shown only as an ideal parallel estimate R/n.

Engineering assumptions and limitations

This approximation assumes a single slender vertical electrode, uniform and isotropic soil, adequate rod length relative to diameter, and no nearby buried metallic services or other electrodes influencing the result. The ideal R/n figure for multiple rods assumes no mutual resistance or spacing interaction and is not a design result.

Important: Actual earth-electrode resistance depends on soil layering and moisture, seasonal variation, electrode depth and geometry, spacing, bonding, corrosion and nearby conductive structures. Use a suitable field test (for example, an appropriate fall-of-potential method) and qualified interpretation. This calculator does not design an earth grid, verify touch/step voltage, fault clearing, or certify compliance with IS/IEC/IEEE or local rules.
