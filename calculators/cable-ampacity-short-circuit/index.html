Apply correction factors to a cable current-carrying capacity taken from a verified manufacturer or applicable standard table. Separately estimate the conductor cross-section required for a short-circuit event using the adiabatic equation and a user-supplied k-factor.

1. Corrected current-carrying capacity

Enter the base ampacity from your chosen cable reference and the applicable correction factors. This tool does not supply ampacity tables.

Enter the ampacity and correction factors from your verified cable data.

2. Short-circuit adiabatic check

Estimate the minimum conductor area from fault current, fault-clearing time and a k-factor supplied from the applicable standard/manufacturer data for the conductor and insulation combination.

Enter verified fault level, clearing time and k-factor.

Equations and engineering conditions

Corrected ampacity: Iz = It × Ca × Cg × Ci × …, where It is the reference current rating and each correction factor is entered separately.

Adiabatic short-circuit equation: S = I × √t / k, where S is conductor area in mm², I is fault current in A, t is clearing time in seconds and k is the applicable material/insulation constant.

Basic overload coordination screen: Ib ≤ In ≤ Iz. This is only a preliminary relationship; protective-device type, trip curve, overload setting, disconnection time, breaking capacity and other design requirements must also be checked.

Critical limitation: no standard ampacity, correction-factor or k-factor tables are embedded. Values must be taken from the exact cable manufacturer's data and the current standard/regulatory rules applicable to the installation. The short-circuit calculation assumes the adiabatic method is applicable for the entered fault duration and conditions. This tool does not verify fault-loop impedance, automatic disconnection, protective-device let-through energy (I²t), conductor temperature limits, parallel conductors, joints or the complete installation. It is not a cable selection approval or a standards-compliance certificate.
