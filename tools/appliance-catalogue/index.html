Publishing checklist

  1. Confirm exact model number and variant from the manufacturer's product page or datasheet.
  2. Verify BEE label and validity period on an official BEE source; leave blank if not applicable or unavailable.
  3. Check retailer price, rating and count on the exact product listing, not a similar variant.
  4. Record the source URL and date for every time-sensitive field.
  5. Review affiliate programme terms and disclosure before publishing tagged links.
  6. Do not publish records marked pending or records with missing evidence.
