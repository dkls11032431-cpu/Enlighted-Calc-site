KNOWLEDGE HUB / BACKUP POWER

UPS and diesel generator systems explained

Understand uninterruptible power supplies, battery autonomy, diesel generator ratings, AMF panels and backup-power maintenance. Reliable backup starts with a load assessment and a clear definition of which circuits must remain powered.

01 / UPS

UPS types and capacity selection

Compare standby, line-interactive and online double-conversion UPS architectures. Selection should consider real load in watts, apparent load in VA, power factor, inrush, crest factor, growth, redundancy and required autonomy.

02 / BATTERIES

Battery capacity and runtime

Runtime depends on load, battery chemistry, DC bus voltage, discharge rate, temperature, age, inverter efficiency and low-voltage cut-off. Simple estimates are indicative; use manufacturer discharge tables and a proper design for final battery selection.

03 / GENERATORS

Diesel generators, AMF and transfer

Understand standby and prime ratings, load steps, alternator capability, starting systems, protection and automatic mains failure sequences. Fuel consumption varies with generator model, load percentage, condition and site conditions.

BACKUP-POWER CHECKLIST

Plan for a reliable changeover

  • List essential loads and identify starting or transient current requirements.
  • Confirm UPS and generator capacity against actual load and required operating mode.
  • Review battery condition, ventilation, temperature, charging and replacement history.
  • Test alarms, interlocks, transfer sequence and return-to-mains operation under an approved procedure.
  • Maintain service records and conduct testing according to manufacturer guidance and applicable regulations.

Frequently asked questions

Can a UPS be selected only by kVA?

No. Check both VA and watt ratings, load power factor, transient behaviour, expansion needs and required backup time.

Why does actual battery runtime differ from a simple calculation?

Battery discharge is not perfectly linear. Age, temperature, discharge rate, efficiency and cut-off voltage all affect usable energy.

Does a generator consume fuel in proportion to its kVA rating?

Fuel use depends on the actual electrical load and the generator's specific fuel-consumption curve. Use the manufacturer's data for the exact model.

UPS batteries, generator systems and transfer panels involve electrical, fire and mechanical hazards. Installation, testing and maintenance must follow manufacturer procedures and be handled by qualified personnel.
