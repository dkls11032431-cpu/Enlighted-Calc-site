MICRO BUSINESS CREDIT

Pradhan Mantri MUDRA Yojana

Loans for eligible non-corporate, non-farm micro and small enterprises through participating lending institutions. Categories include Shishu, Kishor, Tarun and Tarun Plus, subject to current rules and lender assessment.

Official MUDRA website ↗
NEW MICRO-ENTERPRISES

Prime Minister's Employment Generation Programme

Credit-linked margin-money subsidy support for eligible new micro-enterprises. Project eligibility, applicant contribution, subsidy rate and implementing agency depend on current guidelines and applicant category.

Official PMEGP portal ↗
CREDIT GUARANTEE

CGTMSE

Credit guarantee support to eligible lending institutions for eligible micro and small enterprise credit facilities. It is not a direct loan or automatic waiver of repayment.
