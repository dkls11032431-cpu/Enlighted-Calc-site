About Enlighted Technologies

Electrical. Energy. Engineering. Innovation.

Our purpose

Enlighted Technologies is an independent technical knowledge and digital tools initiative focused on making engineering information easier to understand and apply. We cover electrical systems, energy management, solar power, backup power systems, instrumentation and IoT-based monitoring.

What we aim to provide

  • Clear explanations of electrical and energy concepts.
  • Practical calculators with transparent formulas and assumptions.
  • Technical buying guides and product comparisons based on published specifications and clearly identified evaluation criteria.
  • Responsible guidance that recognises the importance of standards, site conditions and qualified professionals.

Our approach

We aim to distinguish general educational information from site-specific engineering advice. Where a topic involves electrical safety, equipment selection or statutory compliance, readers should verify requirements against applicable standards, manufacturer documentation and the actual installation.
