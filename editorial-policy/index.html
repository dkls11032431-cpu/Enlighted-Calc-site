OUR STANDARDS

Editorial Policy

Enlighted Technologies aims to publish clear, useful and responsible technical information.

Accuracy and sourcing

Technical claims should be checked against recognised standards, manufacturer documentation, official publications or other credible references. Where specifications vary by model, installation or jurisdiction, the relevant limitations should be stated.

Practical calculations

Calculators should disclose their formulas, units and key assumptions. Results are estimates for preliminary understanding and must not be treated as certified engineering designs.

Reviews and comparisons

We aim to distinguish published specifications, editorial assessment and hands-on testing. We will not claim that a product was physically tested unless that testing was actually performed.

Corrections

If you identify an error, please contact us with the page URL and supporting details. We will review credible correction requests and update content where warranted.

Safety

Electrical work can cause serious injury, fire or equipment damage. Content is educational and does not replace competent design, inspection, installation or maintenance.
