How our Top 5 lists will work

Each category will have its own shortlist. Front-load and top-load washers, for example, will not be mixed into one ranking. Products will be ranked only after exact-model data is checked; unavailable prices, ratings or star labels will be marked unavailable rather than estimated.

  • Exact model number and variant—not just brand name
  • Current official BEE label and validity period, where the category is covered
  • Specifications, annual energy use and estimated running cost
  • Customer ratings and review counts with source and checked date
  • Warranty, service, price history where available and affiliate disclosure

BEE star ratings: how to verify

BEE ratings are product-class and model-specific. The star label and its validity period can change when standards are revised. Always match the complete model/variant and label period. Do not assume every appliance category is covered by a mandatory or voluntary BEE label.

BEE Standards & Labelling information ↗BEE Star Label app ↗BEE official website ↗

Live-data status: BEE states that its consumer app is linked to the BEE Standards & Labelling database and that app data is updated daily. We have not confirmed a public website API or reuse permission for bulk model data. Until an authorised feed or dataset is available, this site will link to the official lookup and show model-level ratings only after manual verification.

Editorial standard: no fabricated Top 5 lists, product prices, BEE stars, ratings, review counts or claims of hands-on testing. Product and scheme information is time-sensitive and will show source and verification date when published.
