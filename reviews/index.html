TECHNICAL BUYING CHECKLISTS

What matters before
you spend your money.

These are evaluation criteria, not product endorsements. Confirm the exact model's current specification sheet and applicable Indian label or safety requirements.

Refrigerator

  • Choose capacity for household size and shopping frequency.
  • Compare BEE label validity period and annual energy consumption in kWh.
  • Check compressor type, climate class, stabiliser requirement and usable freezer space.
  • Review door gasket, shelf strength, noise, service coverage and warranty exclusions.

Washing machine

  • Match rated capacity to family size and laundry frequency.
  • Compare front-load/top-load wash performance, water use and spin speed.
  • Check motor warranty separately from comprehensive product warranty.
  • Look for recurring feedback on vibration, bearings, drain pumps and service.

Iron box

  • Compare dry/steam type, rated wattage and thermostat control.
  • Check soleplate coating, cord flexibility, leakage protection and auto shut-off where offered.
  • Read recurring feedback about temperature consistency, sticking and water leakage.

Geyser / water heater

  • Select storage capacity based on use and outlet flow.
  • Verify pressure rating for high-rise or pressure-pump installations.
  • Check thermostat, thermal cut-out, pressure relief valve, earthing and corrosion protection.
  • Review installation conditions, anode maintenance and tank warranty.

Microwave oven

  • Choose solo, grill or convection based on actual cooking needs.
  • Compare cavity capacity, microwave output power and rated input power separately.
  • Check turntable size, controls, ventilation clearance and serviceability.

Air conditioner

  • Size by room heat load, orientation, occupancy and insulation—not floor area alone.
  • Compare cooling capacity, ISEER, annual energy consumption and operating range.
  • Include installation, copper piping, stabiliser and service costs in total ownership.

Water purifier

  • Test source-water quality before choosing RO, UV or UF technology.
  • Compare recovery ratio, reject-water volume, storage and filter replacement cost.
  • Check service availability and consumable pricing in your area.

Mixer grinder

  • Match motor rating and jar configuration to intended tasks.
  • Check overload protection, coupler design, jar lids and spare-part availability.
  • Look for recurring reports of overheating, leakage and noise.

Ceiling / BLDC fan

  • Compare air delivery, service value and rated input power at relevant speed.
  • Check sweep size, downrod, regulator/remote compatibility and warranty.
  • Compare electricity use at your normal operating speed.

Dishwasher

  • Choose place settings for actual daily utensil volume.
  • Compare water and energy use per cycle, programme options and noise.
  • Check inlet-water requirements, detergent availability and local service support.
