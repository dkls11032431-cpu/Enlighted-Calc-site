BEFORE YOU SIZE A SYSTEM

Rooftop solar planning checklist

  • Review at least 12 months of electricity bills and note monthly and daytime consumption.
  • Check usable roof area, shading, orientation, structural suitability and access for maintenance.
  • Decide whether the goal is bill reduction, backup during outages or off-grid operation.
  • Compare inverter type, warranty, service support, protections and monitoring features.
  • Confirm current net-metering or grid-interconnection rules with the local distribution utility.

Frequently asked questions

How much energy does a rooftop solar system generate?

Generation varies with location, weather, orientation, shading, module temperature, system design and losses. Use a site-specific estimate rather than a single universal daily-generation figure.

Does a grid-tied solar system provide backup during a power cut?

Many standard grid-tied systems shut down during a grid outage for anti-islanding safety. Backup requires compatible equipment and a properly designed backup arrangement.

Is a larger battery always better?

No. Battery capacity should match the essential loads, required backup duration, inverter limits and budget. Oversizing without a load assessment can add unnecessary cost.

Solar designs and grid connections must follow current local regulations, utility requirements and manufacturer instructions. Have the roof, electrical installation and proposed system assessed by qualified professionals.
