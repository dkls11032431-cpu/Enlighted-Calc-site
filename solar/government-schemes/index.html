Rooftop solar subsidy in Tamil Nadu

A practical guide for residential consumers exploring PM Surya Ghar and the Tamil Nadu rooftop-solar assistance shown on the state portal. Scheme rules, funding and portal procedures can change; verify eligibility and the live application status before paying a vendor.

What the Tamil Nadu portal currently displays

The TN Solar Homes Portal displays central assistance of up to ₹78,000 plus a Tamil Nadu state top-up of up to ₹22,000, for total assistance up to ₹1,00,000 for eligible residential rooftop systems. The portal's displayed capacity table is shown below. The state portal also notes that state assistance is subject to eligibility, installation, net-metering, commissioning and central CFA disbursal.

Who should check eligibility?

  • Residential electricity consumers applying for grid-connected rooftop solar.
  • Applicants should have a valid electricity consumer account and meet the current national and state scheme conditions.
  • Use the current portal's eligible-vendor list and confirm the proposed modules meet applicable DCR/ALMM requirements.
  • Do not assume commercial, industrial or non-residential connections qualify for residential CFA.

How to apply in Tamil Nadu

1. Register on the national PM Surya Ghar portal

Use your state, electricity distribution company and consumer account number to register and submit the rooftop-solar application. Keep the generated application/reference number.

Open PM Surya Ghar National Portal ↗

2. Complete the TNPDCL / TN Solar Homes portal process

Use the national portal reference number in the Tamil Nadu Unified Solar Rooftop Portal and complete the utility-side application and applicable charges/feasibility steps shown there.

Open Tamil Nadu Solar Rooftop Portal ↗

3. Select an eligible vendor and agree the scope

Check the current portal's empanelled-vendor list. Get a written quotation covering module make/model, inverter, structure, protections, earthing, cabling, generation monitoring, warranty, installation and taxes. Compare at least two quotations.

4. Install, inspect and commission

Complete installation as per the approved process. Upload required plant, inverter, module and DCR details. Coordinate utility inspection, meter/net-metering and commissioning as directed by TNPDCL.

5. Claim and track the subsidy

Submit bank details and required documents on the national portal. Track the central CFA claim there. The TN portal describes the state top-up process as following central disbursal and verification; check the current state portal for actual eligibility, timelines and status.

Documents and information to keep ready

  • Latest electricity bill and consumer/account number
  • Applicant identity and bank-account details as requested by the portal
  • Mobile number linked to application and portal reference number
  • Vendor quotation, equipment details, installation photographs and commissioning records
  • Any declarations or documents specifically requested by the live portal

Important checks before paying

  • Do not pay based only on a verbal promise of subsidy or a claim of guaranteed free electricity.
  • Confirm vendor registration on the official portal on the day you sign.
  • Read the current scheme guidelines and TNPDCL technical/utility requirements.
  • Confirm whether the quotation is before or after subsidy; subsidy is not necessarily an upfront vendor discount.
  • Check roof structure, shading, sanctioned load, phase, inverter capacity and protection design with qualified professionals.

Official sources and update status

  • TN Solar Homes / TNPDCL portal — state portal's current scheme information, application and vendor resources.
  • TN portal FAQ — application route, consumer process and utility guidance.
  • Ministry of New and Renewable Energy — central scheme guidelines and amendments.
  • PM Surya Ghar National Portal — registration, vendor selection and application tracking.
