PRACTICAL CHECKLIST

What to verify in an electrical system

  • Confirm the supply type, rated voltage, connected load and expected demand.
  • Check that conductor sizing and protective-device ratings are supported by a proper design calculation.
  • Verify protective earthing, bonding and residual-current protection where required.
  • For metering, document meter wiring, CT ratio, polarity and phase mapping.
  • Investigate recurring trips, overheating, unusual voltage variation or imbalance through a qualified electrician.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between kW and kWh?

kW measures the rate of using or producing real power. kWh measures electrical energy accumulated over time.

Why is CT polarity important?

CT polarity and phase association affect the direction and accuracy of measured current and power. Follow the meter and CT manufacturer's wiring instructions.

Can an online guide replace an electrical inspection?

No. This material is educational. Inspection, design and electrical work should be performed by qualified persons using applicable standards and site-specific information.
